# Low Water: how to play

A very slow tide pool you can't control.

Ten rocks and a tide. Things grow when the water is in, and the only thing you decide is what
not to disturb. The pool keeps real time: come back after a week away and it has changed.
Nothing here can be won, lost or hurried.

## The clock

The tide takes 12 hours 25 minutes, high water to high water, counted from the date and time of
your device. While you are away the tides go on: fourteen of them in a week. When you come back
the pool is brought up to date tide by tide (at most 2000 of them, nearly three years) and you are
told what happened.

The moon sets the range: at new and full moon the spring tides rise higher and fall lower; at the
quarters the neap tides hardly move. The sun, the moon with its phase, the season and the weather
all come from the real date.

## The seasons

Winter (December to February): growth is halved, little settles, storms are common. Spring:
things settle eagerly. Summer: growth is a quarter faster, storms are rare, crabs wander in.
Autumn: storms return.

## What lives there

| | |
|---|---|
| algae | a slick film on bare rock; the start of everything |
| barnacles | slow, and tough; they feed when the water covers them |
| kelp | fast; a full grown stand may let go; urchins find it |
| mussels | slow; a sea star finds them on a rock that stays quiet |
| anemone | opens on a barnacle rock left quiet for 12 days |
| sea star | arrives, eats, moves on |
| hermit crab, urchin | visitors; they cross a rock and go |

A storm tide now and then tears barnacles, kelp and mussels off. Everything has four sizes:
tiny, young, grown, full grown.

## What you decide

You have five cairn stones. Tap a rock, then **Place a cairn** (or lift it). A rock with a cairn
is left alone.

When you leave, you wade out across the rocks that have no cairn: what lives there is stepped on,
set back a quarter and a little more, and its quiet starts again from nothing; visitors are scared
off. You have left when the app has been out of sight for more than ten minutes; a glance at
another app costs nothing.

The rare things (anemones, sea stars) need quiet: days of it. Visit often and walk the same rocks
and only the common things win. Choose the five you will leave alone, and go away for a while.

## Hands and keys

| | |
|---|---|
| tap a rock | look at it: what lives on it, how long it has been quiet |
| drag sideways | move along the pool (on a narrow screen) |
| the pencil | name the rock |
| the book | the diary: what the pool did, newest first |
| the waves, or the tide chip | the tide table: the next day of the tide |
| the sun | settings: language, sound, the tide bell, a new pool |
| left/right, 1-9 and 0 | choose a rock |
| Space or Enter | put a cairn on it, or lift it |
| N, T, J, B, ? | name, tides, diary (journal), bell, the first words |

The pool is kept in the browser's storage on this device.
